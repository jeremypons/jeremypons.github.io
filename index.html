Why payments? Because nobody dreams about them.

Nobody wakes up wanting to change how they pay. That is exactly why it needs the right words.

I studied brand and marketing because everything in life runs on experience, recognition and the right words. Dublin put me next to the SaaS giants and showed me the dot that connects brand, investment and go-to-market: the product.

Years of freelance work across industries taught me the rest. Companies spend too much or too little on brand. Product is what you build, sell and give to the world. And the products a society can’t run without, in banking and healthcare, deserve the best packaging there is.

So I went where those products live. Helping banks and insurers go API-first and to the cloud. Implementing PSD2 inside banks at the birth of open banking. Then six years at a global open banking leader, through partnerships, consumer growth, product marketing, a region’s commercial engine, and now the brand.

Before we talk

What roles are you open to?

C-level roles across marketing, product and sales, plus board, investor and founder advisory. Payments, fintech, AI and B2B SaaS, internationally.

Where are you based?

Stockholm. Remote-ready, and on a plane a few times a month for the rooms that matter. At home in the Nordics and France, and working with the US fintech market.

B2B or B2C?

Strongest in B2B and SaaS, and comfortable running the consumer side of a B2B2C model, where the merchant buys and the consumer decides.

How do you use AI?

Every day, and in every team I lead. I help put the frameworks and policies in place for marketing, sales and product, so AI improves the work instead of decorating it.

Why one leader across three disciplines?

Because growth rarely dies inside a team. It dies between them. One owner of the story, the product and the number removes the handoffs where it leaks.

Building the next growth engine, preparing a board, or looking for a speaker on payments and AI? Based in Stockholm, remote-ready, on a plane a few times a month.
